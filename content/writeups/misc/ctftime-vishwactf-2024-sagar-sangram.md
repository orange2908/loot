### Solution:   
We are provided with a Discord server link. The instructions say we need to answer a few questions to get the flag.

From the description, which mentions the churning of the ocean, a Google search reveals the event refers to "Samudra Manthana."

The bot's questions are based on events that occurred during the Samudra Manthana. The answers to the bot's questions can be derived from this event.
