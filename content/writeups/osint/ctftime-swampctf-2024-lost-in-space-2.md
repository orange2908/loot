> I think OSINT challenges are stupid! If they aren’t, prove it! How far away is this? Don’t bother giving me the unit, there’s only one that you should be using in space anyways (use Astronomical Units). The flag entry is extremely forgiving just make sure you get the integer for distance right.

This a classic OSINT image question. We search the image with google image search.

After a search, we learned that this satellite is Voyager 2. In the question, we are asked about its distance from the Earth and we can reach the result with a simple google search.
